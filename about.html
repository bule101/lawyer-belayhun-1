The advocate
An advocate who explains before he argues.

Clients arrive with a deadline, a document they do not fully understand, or a dispute that is costing them sleep. Our first duty is to tell you plainly where you stand and what each option will cost in time and money.

Adv. Belayhun represents individuals, family businesses, startups and foreign investors before federal and Addis Ababa courts, and negotiates settlements wherever that serves the client better than a hearing.

  • LL.B., Addis Ababa University School of Law
  • Licensed advocate, Ethiopia
  • Member, Ethiopian Bar Association
  • Working languages: Amharic, English
