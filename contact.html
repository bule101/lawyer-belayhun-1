FAQ

Common questions

How much does a consultation cost?

Fees depend on the service and are confirmed when the office replies to your booking, before we meet.

Can I meet in Amharic or English?

Yes. Consultations run in Amharic or English, as you prefer.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. Everything you share is protected by advocate–client confidentiality.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. Message the office with your reference number and it will be moved.

Do you take cases outside Addis Ababa?

Yes, by arrangement. Phone and video consultations are available from anywhere.
